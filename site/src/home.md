# Know what your car really costs to run

Compare battery-electric, plug-in hybrid, hybrid and petrol vehicles using **your** mileage and **your** local energy prices — not a single national average.

## What the calculator will do

- **Home vs public fast charging, priced separately.** Charging in your garage and paying for DC fast charging are different economics; mixing them hides the truth.
- **Per-model results.** Efficiency differs a lot between a Model Y, a Bolt and an F-150 Lightning.
- **Per-state electricity prices**, so the answer reflects where you actually live.
- **EV vs PHEV vs hybrid vs petrol**, on the same assumptions, side by side.
- **The formula is on the page.** You should be able to check our arithmetic, not just trust it.

## How the estimate is worked out

```
monthly cost = (monthly miles ÷ efficiency in mi/kWh)
               × price in $/kWh
               × (1 + charging loss %)
```

Home and public charging are weighted separately because they are priced differently. Fuel comparisons use miles ÷ MPG × fuel price, adjusted for city/highway split; plug-in hybrids use the EPA utility factor for the share of miles that actually run on electricity.

## Where the numbers come from

| Data | Source |
|---|---|
| Vehicle efficiency (mi/kWh, MPGe, MPG) | U.S. EPA / fueleconomy.gov |
| Plug-in hybrid utility factor | U.S. EPA |
| State electricity prices | U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) |
| Fuel prices | U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) |
| Public DC fast-charging prices | Publicly published network pricing |

Every results page will show **when its rates were last updated**, because stale rates are worse than no rates.

## What you will not find here

No signup, no account, no paywall, no uploads. No charging-station map, no route planning, and no total-cost-of-ownership modelling — this is a **running-cost** tool. Nothing is generated by AI.

The calculator runs **in your browser**. The numbers you type in are not sent to our servers. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
