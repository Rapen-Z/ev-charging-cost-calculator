# Disclaimer

**Last updated:** 2026-09-10

## This is an estimate, not a bill

Every number on this site is an **estimate** produced from published average data and the assumptions shown on the page. It is **not** a quote, **not** a prediction of your next electricity or fuel bill, and **not** financial, legal, tax, or engineering advice.

## What the numbers depend on

Your real-world cost varies with things we cannot know about you, including:

- Your utility's specific tariff, including time-of-use windows, tiers, fixed charges, and taxes
- Whether you are on a special EV or off-peak rate plan
- Outdoor temperature and season (cold weather reduces efficiency and increases consumption)
- Your driving style, speed, terrain, and payload
- Battery condition and age, and charger efficiency losses
- The specific pricing, idle fees, and subscription terms of any public charging network
- Local fuel prices if you are comparing against a petrol or diesel vehicle

Treat our output as a **starting point for comparison**, not as a number to budget against.

## Where our data comes from

We compile figures from public sources, including data published by the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA), the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) and fueleconomy.gov, and publicly published charging-network pricing pages.

Each page shows **when its rates were last updated**. We do not claim the data is real-time, and we do not claim our output is exact or "100% accurate".
