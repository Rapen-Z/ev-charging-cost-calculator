# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-09-10

## The short version

- The calculator runs **entirely in your browser**. The numbers you type in are not sent to our servers and we do not store them.
- We do **not** ask for your name, email address, phone number, or payment details. There is no account and no signup.
- Our hosting provider does record standard technical information (such as your IP address and the pages you request), as explained below. We do not claim to collect "nothing" — we explain exactly what is collected.
- If you share a result link, the inputs travel **in the link itself**. See [Shared links](#shared-links).

## Information we handle

### 1. Information you type into the calculator

Depending on the page you use, this may include:

| What | Examples | Where it stays |
|---|---|---|
| Location | **U.S. state** derived from a ZIP code you enter | Your browser only |
| Energy prices | Electricity rate ($/kWh), fuel price | Your browser only |
| Driving habits | Monthly mileage, city/highway split | Your browser only |
| Charging setup | Home vs. public fast-charging split; whether home charging is available | Your browser only |
| Vehicle | Selected make/model; fuel type | Your browser only |

This information is processed **locally in your browser** to produce an estimate. It is not transmitted to us and we do not store it on a server.

### 2. Shared links

Some pages let you copy a link that reproduces your result. The link contains the inputs as URL parameters (for example the state, mileage and rate values).

Please be aware that:

- Anyone you send the link to can see those values.
- URL parameters may appear in the technical logs of our hosting provider.
- URL parameters may be sent as a `Referer` header if you follow an outbound link from a shared page.

To limit this, we deliberately **do not put your raw ZIP code into shareable links** — we store the resulting U.S. state instead. We also set a restrictive `Referrer-Policy` so that other sites do not receive the full address you came from.

### 3. Information collected automatically

When you request a page, our hosting and content-delivery provider (**Cloudflare**) necessarily receives and processes technical data:

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| IP address | Delivering the page, security, abuse and bot protection |
| User agent and device/browser hints | Rendering correctly, detecting abuse |
| Referring page | Understanding traffic sources, detecting abuse |
| Requested URL (including any query string) and timestamp | Serving the request, diagnosing errors, security |

Our lawful basis for this processing is our **legitimate interest** in delivering a working, secure website (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) / UK GDPR equivalent).

### 5. Information we do NOT collect

- No account credentials — there are no accounts.
- No payment data — nothing on this site is sold.
- No uploads — the site accepts no file uploads.
- No automated profiling — we do not run generative models on your inputs.
- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (see [Children](#children)).

## Children

This site is a cost-estimation tool for adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Data retention

- Calculator inputs: **not retained** — they stay in your browser session.
- Cookie consent record: kept so we can demonstrate your choice; expiring after **12 months**, after which you will be asked again.
- Server/security logs: retained according to our hosting provider's configuration (see [§3](#3-information-collected-automatically)).
